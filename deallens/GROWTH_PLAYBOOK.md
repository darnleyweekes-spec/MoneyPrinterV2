# DealLens Growth Mode

MoneyPrinterV2 is used here as a separate AGPL-licensed growth/distribution engine. It is not embedded into the proprietary DealLens diligence product.

## Objective

Generate qualified attention from small acquisition teams and convert it into DealLens Express engagements at $1,500 per deal.

Primary site:
https://darnleyweekes-spec.github.io/jarvis-workforce-hq/deallens/

Redacted sample:
https://darnleyweekes-spec.github.io/jarvis-workforce-hq/deallens/sample.html

## ICP

Prioritize:
- search funds
- independent sponsors
- boutique M&A advisory firms
- family offices with direct acquisition activity
- lower-middle-market private equity teams

Avoid broad consumer traffic. A small number of qualified buyers is more valuable than large generic reach.

## Conversion funnel

1. Short educational post/video names a real acquisition-diligence risk.
2. CTA sends the viewer to the DealLens redacted sample.
3. Site CTA offers DealLens Express at $1,500 per deal.
4. Qualified prospect replies or emails.
5. Human discovery confirms fit and scope before confidential materials are accepted.

## Content pillars

### Customer concentration
- Why one customer at 30%+ can dominate your underwriting case
- Questions to ask when the top five customers exceed 50% of revenue
- Change-of-control and relationship-owner risk

### CIM skepticism
- Three claims in a CIM that deserve source evidence
- Growth rate versus quality of growth
- Adjusted EBITDA: what to normalize before believing it

### First-pass finance
- Revenue growth is not enough: margin and liquidity checks
- Net debt versus cash at close
- Working-capital questions before deeper QoE spend

### Management questions
- Five questions to ask before paying for full diligence
- What a concentration table should trigger in management diligence
- What declining revenue should force management to reconcile

### AI + diligence trust
- Why an LLM should not be allowed to invent deal arithmetic
- How source-linked evidence makes AI diligence more reviewable
- Why uploaded documents should be treated as untrusted input

## Publishing rules

- No fabricated deal examples presented as real.
- Demo numbers must be labeled demo/redacted/sample data.
- No guaranteed investment returns or claims that DealLens replaces professional diligence.
- Do not disclose confidential prospect or client information.
- Do not automate high-volume spam. Targeted outreach must stay relevant and reviewable.
- Every post should have one CTA only: view sample, request sample, or discuss a deal.

## Success metrics

Track in this order:
1. qualified replies
2. discovery calls / substantive email conversations
3. sample views from target firms
4. paid DealLens Express engagements
5. content impressions/views

Views are a leading indicator, not the business outcome.
